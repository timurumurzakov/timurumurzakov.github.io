About

I am a PhD in Robotics Engineering specializing in humanoid robotics, control systems, neural-network-based modeling, and robotic simulation. My research focuses on energy-efficient humanoid locomotion, compliant actuation, and neural-augmented dynamic modeling.

I work on the development of lightweight humanoid robots, elastic actuators, and ANN-based control frameworks using simulation platforms such as Isaac Sim/Lab and PyBullet.
